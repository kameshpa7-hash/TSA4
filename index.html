Laboratory Concept & Algorithm Formulation

Theoretical foundation of Extractive Text Summarization

NLP Theory
💡
Definition:

“Text summarization automatically reduces a long document into a shorter version while preserving its important information.”

Extractive vs. Abstractive Summarization

This project implements an extractive summarization algorithm. Unlike abstractive summarization (which generates brand-new paraphrased sentences using heavy language models), extractive summarization scores, extracts, and combines the most statistically significant original sentences directly from the source passage. This guarantees 100% factual accuracy, zero hallucination, and high computational efficiency.

The 7-Step Extractive Summarization Pipeline

1

Sentence Segmentation

Splits continuous raw text into distinct sentence units using regular expression boundary matching on punctuation (., !, ?), while protecting numerical figures and common abbreviations.

2

Word Tokenization

Isolates individual words from each sentence, converts characters to lowercase, and strips away punctuation marks, brackets, and extraneous symbols.

3

Stop Word Filtering

Discards frequent grammatical glue words (e.g., the, is, at, which, on, and, have) using an exhaustive English stop words lexicon so only semantically informative content words remain.

4

Word Frequency Calculation

Computes the term frequency \( f(w) \) of each retained word across the passage, and normalizes it relative to the most frequent word: \( W_{norm}(w) = \frac{f(w)}{f_{max}} \).

5

Sentence Scoring

Calculates each sentence's significance score by summing the weights of its constituent content words, balanced by sentence length normalization to avoid bias toward disproportionately long run-on sentences.

6

Top-K Sentence Selection

Sorts all sentences by their calculated significance score in descending order, and extracts the top \( K \) sentences according to the user-selected summary length (Short, Medium, or Long).

7

Chronological Order Preservation

Re-sorts the extracted top sentences according to their original sequential position in the source text. This preserves narrative cohesion, logical progression, and conversational flow in the final summary.
